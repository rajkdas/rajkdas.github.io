Fit check

Best for businesses with a repeated question, event or action worth bringing into chat.

Strong fit

  • You have a SaaS, API, data product, internal system, CRM, support tool, booking platform or e-commerce operation.
  • Users or staff repeatedly check data, request updates, make simple decisions or react to important events.
  • You need a specific workflow to cross tools reliably rather than another manual step for your team.
  • You want a product feature or operational capability that works in the channels your audience already uses.

Probably not needed

  • You only need a basic static FAQ responder or a one-way broadcast channel.
  • You do not yet have a repeatable process, source data or clear customer/team need to automate.
  • You expect an unconstrained AI to make high-risk decisions without business rules, permissions or review.
