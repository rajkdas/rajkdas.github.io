Telegram gives your product a direct, low-friction surface. A well-designed bot can pull users back with alerts, make data instantly available and enable fast actions—while your backend remains the source of truth.

Fit check

Best for products with data, events or repeat actions worth bringing into chat.

Strong fit

  • You run a SaaS, API, data product, community, trading tool, monitoring product or internal business system.
  • Your users or team repeatedly check data, status, reports or alerts in a dashboard.
  • You have one or more APIs, databases or workflows a bot can safely connect to.
  • You want a differentiated product feature that increases accessibility and repeat usage.

Probably not needed

  • You only need a basic broadcast channel without user interaction or automation.
  • You do not have a defined data source, business workflow or customer problem for the bot yet.
  • You expect a bot to make unrestricted high-risk financial, medical or legal decisions without review.
